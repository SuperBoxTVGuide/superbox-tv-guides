# SuperBox TV Guides

Setup tips and troubleshooting resources for SuperBox TV devices.

## Basic Setup

1. Connect your SuperBox to your TV with an HDMI cable.
2. Connect the power adapter and select the correct HDMI input on your TV.
3. Connect the device to Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish setup.
